## Headline finding
A real `dnf` transaction with systemd `%post` scriptlets runs correctly **inside the
image** while being unable to signal, mount, or `systemctl` anything on the
**controller** — the precise failure class that motivated replacing `lchroot`. The
PID-isolation contrast (5 vs 675 visible PIDs) is the clearest single piece of
evidence that the kill-vector is closed.

## New holes → feedback loop
- **TMPDIR leak** (S8): logged and fixed same-day; regression-guarded by S8 here and
  by `test_sandbox.py::test_rw_argv_has_all_hardening` (asserts `--setenv TMPDIR /tmp`).
  Recorded as JOURNAL.md L13 and `src/agent/MEMORY.md`.
- **No cgroup limits** (S10): recorded as a known gap; candidate for an opt-in
  `--limits` flag wrapping `systemd-run --scope`.
